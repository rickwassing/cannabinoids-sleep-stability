# Phase 4 — Manuscript restructuring

## Purpose

Rewrite and reorganize the manuscript so that readers can understand the biological questions and findings without unnecessary signal-processing detail in Results, while preserving sufficient context in the Results-before-Methods version.

## Reviewer concerns covered

This phase addresses:

- The Results section being difficult to follow and overly technical
- Insufficient intuitive explanation of ISFσ phase and its functional significance
- FIR and other implementation details appearing in Results
- Definitions being confined to supplementary material
- Confusing reporting of the REM half-period models
- Missing definitions of clinical sleep measures
- Insufficient explanation of sample-size differences and study procedures
- Placement of new sensitivity analyses and revised limitations

## High-level workstreams

1. **Restructure Results around biological questions**
   - Organize each subsection around the question, principal finding, quantitative evidence, and biological meaning.

2. **Consolidate technical detail in Methods**
   - Move implementation details, model construction, filtering, transforms, corrections, and statistical procedures to the appropriate Methods subsections.

3. **Retain minimal forward context in the IRDM version**
   - Provide concise, intuitive explanations needed to understand Results that appear before Methods without reproducing full methodology.

4. **Introduce clear definitions**
   - Define ISFσ concepts, phase-distribution language, clinical sleep metrics, arousal outcomes, and REM-transition measures at first use.

5. **Clarify reporting of complex models**
   - Present interactions and follow-up effects in a logical order, especially for sleep-stage and REM-transition findings.

6. **Integrate new analyses and qualifications**
   - Add the principal Phase 2 findings to Results and place detailed robustness outputs in supplementary material.
   - Add corresponding limitations and interpretive changes from Phase 3.

7. **Revise abstract, significance statement, and conclusion**
   - Ensure that high-level claims reflect the complete objective, subjective, sensitivity, and healthy-control evidence.

## Expected outputs

- A revised section architecture for both manuscript formats
- Clear Results subsections centered on biological findings
- Consolidated and complete Methods descriptions
- Intuitive definitions and signposting
- Revised abstract, significance statement, limitations, and conclusion
- A placement map for main-text and supplementary material

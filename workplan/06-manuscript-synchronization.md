# Phase 6 — Manuscript synchronization

## Purpose

Ensure that the IMRD and IRDM manuscript variants contain identical scientific content after the analyses, interpretation, restructuring, and figure revisions are complete.

## Scope

This phase covers synchronization of:

- Numerical results and sample sizes
- Definitions and terminology
- Methods and operational rules
- Biological and clinical interpretations
- Limitations and qualifications
- Abstract and significance statement
- Tables, figures, legends, and supplementary references
- Citations and bibliography
- Healthy-control material
- Reviewer-requested additions

## High-level workstreams

1. **Establish canonical scientific content**
   - Identify the authoritative wording and results for each manuscript component before adapting section order.

2. **Compare the two variants systematically**
   - Detect differences beyond the intended section order and limited forward explanation.

3. **Resolve section-order adaptations**
   - Preserve sufficient context in the IRDM Results while avoiding unnecessary duplication or inconsistent methodological wording.

4. **Verify cross-references**
   - Check headings, figure and table numbers, supplementary references, citations, and internal references in both versions.

5. **Verify scientific equivalence**
   - Confirm that every estimate, conclusion, caveat, and definition has the same meaning in both files.

6. **Define an ongoing synchronization process**
   - Establish how future changes will be propagated to prevent the variants from diverging again.

## Expected outputs

- Two scientifically equivalent manuscript variants
- A documented list of intentional order-related differences
- Resolved cross-references and consistent numbering
- A final synchronization check or comparison report
- A workflow for maintaining equivalence through submission

## To be specified in the phase-specific chat

- Choice of canonical source or synchronization method
- Rules for permitted differences
- Comparison and validation procedure
- Handling of journal-specific formatting
- Acceptance criteria for scientific and editorial equivalence

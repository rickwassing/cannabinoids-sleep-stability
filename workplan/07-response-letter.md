# Phase 7 — Point-by-point response letter

## Purpose

Prepare a complete and persuasive response letter that answers every distinct concern and question raised by both reviewers and directs them to the corresponding analyses and manuscript revisions.

## Scope

The response must cover:

- All major and minor comments from Reviewer 1
- The healthy-control concern and all numbered comments from Reviewer 2
- Every sub-question embedded within compound comments
- New analyses, sensitivity analyses, and null results
- Changes in interpretation and strength of claims
- Manuscript restructuring and definitions
- Figure and accessibility revisions
- Locations of all changes in the submitted manuscript

## High-level workstreams

1. **Create a complete comment register**
   - Decompose each reviewer comment into its individual requests, questions, and conceptual concerns.

2. **Map each concern to completed work**
   - Link comments to analyses, manuscript edits, figures, supplementary outputs, or reasoned responses.

3. **Draft point-by-point responses**
   - Acknowledge the concern, answer it directly, summarize the evidence, explain what changed, and identify manuscript locations.

4. **Report new analyses transparently**
   - Include principal estimates and conclusions whether they support, qualify, or contradict the original interpretation.

5. **Explain decisions not to follow a suggestion literally**
   - Where an alternative approach is more defensible, explain the rationale respectfully and show how the underlying concern was addressed.

6. **Maintain consistency with both manuscript variants**
   - Prepare location references for the version being submitted while ensuring the scientific response remains applicable to both.

7. **Perform a completeness and tone review**
   - Ensure no sub-question is omitted, no response overstates the evidence, and the overall tone is appreciative and constructive.

## Expected outputs

- A reviewer-comment register with all compound comments decomposed
- A complete point-by-point response letter
- Exact manuscript and supplementary location references
- Concise summaries of all added analyses and revisions
- A final completeness check linking every reviewer concern to a response and revision

# Phase 3 — Interpretation revision

## Purpose

Revise the biological, pharmacological, clinical, and therapeutic interpretation so that it is informative, internally coherent, and appropriately bounded by what was directly measured.

## Reviewer concerns covered

This phase addresses comments concerning:

- How cortical arousals can occur during an ascending ISFσ phase associated with relatively low LC–NA activity
- Alternative noradrenergic and non-noradrenergic mechanisms
- Tonic versus phasic LC activity
- The absence of global treatment effects on ISFσ frequency, amplitude, and heart-rate coupling
- Regional heterogeneity in ISFσ properties
- Opposite treatment effects in N1 and N2
- Continued-sleep arousals during the descending phase under placebo
- Interpretation of reduced pre-REM ISFσ amplitude and delayed REM
- Dose-dependent and compound-specific effects of THC and CBD
- The mismatch between stage-specific stability findings and worse overall objective sleep
- The limits of therapeutic claims
- The implications and limits of healthy-control findings

## High-level workstreams

1. **Define the inferential role of ISFσ**
   - Distinguish an indirect LC–NA-linked EEG proxy from direct measurement of LC firing or cortical noradrenaline.

2. **Develop a nuanced arousal framework**
   - Separate arousal generation, phase-dependent susceptibility, and progression from cortical arousal to sustained wakefulness.
   - Incorporate tonic/phasic distinctions and other arousal systems.

3. **Reconcile local, global, and state-specific findings**
   - Explain how event-specific or pre-REM findings can coexist with null all-night ISFσ findings.
   - Address regional expression without treating scalp topography as a direct map of LC activity.

4. **Reconcile sleep-stage effects**
   - Develop a cautious account of why the net treatment effect may differ between N1, N2, and REM regulation.

5. **Contextualize the cannabinoid preparation**
   - Describe what can and cannot be inferred about THC, CBD, their combination, dose, route, exposure, and receptor location.

6. **Reframe clinical and therapeutic implications**
   - Distinguish mechanistic insight from clinical efficacy.
   - Integrate objective and subjective sleep outcomes without overstating benefit.

7. **Integrate healthy-control interpretation**
   - State which conclusions concern insomnia-related differences, which concern treatment effects, and which cannot be separated with the available design.

## Expected outputs

- A revised conceptual framework for the Introduction and Discussion
- A hierarchy separating observations, supported interpretations, plausible alternatives, and unresolved mechanisms
- A list of claims to retain, qualify, replace, or remove
- A literature map supporting the revised interpretation
- Updated conclusions and significance framing for use in Phase 4

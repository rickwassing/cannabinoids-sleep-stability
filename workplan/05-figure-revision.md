# Phase 5 — Figure revision

## Purpose

Revise the main and supplementary figures so that they communicate the verified findings clearly, match the statistical analyses, and remain accessible to readers with color-vision deficiencies.

## Reviewer concerns covered

This phase addresses:

- Difficulty interpreting Figure 4E–F
- Questions about the apparent pre-REM dip and post-transition increase in Figure 4D
- Similar colors for treatment and transition-to-REM fluctuation categories
- Red/green-like combinations that are difficult for colorblind readers
- Absence of visible statistical-significance indicators
- Need for a clearer explanation of ISFσ phase
- Presentation of new sensitivity and time-of-night analyses

## High-level workstreams

1. **Adopt a consistent accessible visual system**
   - Define treatment colors, fluctuation-type colors, line styles, markers, and labels that work in color and grayscale.

2. **Clarify Figure 4**
   - Redesign relationship panels so that observations, model estimates, uncertainty, fluctuation type, and treatment are distinguishable.
   - Clearly separate pre-REM and post-transition inference.

3. **Add appropriate inferential annotations**
   - Mark tested comparisons, corrected time intervals, interactions, or significant clusters without adding misleading or excessive symbols.

4. **Add an intuitive ISFσ phase schematic**
   - Visually define trough, ascending phase, peak, descending phase, and the cautious LC–NA-linked interpretation.

5. **Integrate new analysis displays**
   - Determine which Phase 2 results belong in main figures and which should appear in supplementary figures or tables.

6. **Align figures with inferential models**
   - Ensure plotted lines, summaries, uncertainty intervals, and annotations correspond to the models reported in the manuscript.

7. **Review all legends**
   - Make each legend sufficiently explanatory and define symbols, abbreviations, sample sizes, time windows, and multiplicity correction.

## Expected outputs

- A consistent colorblind-safe figure style
- Revised Figure 4 and any other affected main figures
- An ISFσ phase explanatory schematic
- Statistical annotations tied to reported tests
- Supplementary figures for sensitivity analyses where needed
- Updated, self-contained figure legends
